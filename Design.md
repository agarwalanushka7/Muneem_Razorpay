# AI Revenue Agent — Design System

## 1. Design Goal

The interface should feel:

- Professional
- Modern
- Trustworthy
- Financial
- AI-powered
- Clean
- Easy to understand

The merchant should immediately understand:

1. What is happening in their business.
2. What the AI has discovered.
3. What action the AI recommends.
4. Whether the merchant needs to approve anything.

The interface should prioritize clarity over visual decoration.

---

The product is merchant-first.
